# Activity 04 · Your AI usage charter

In this activity, you will write your own AI usage charter: five commitments, in your own words, that say how you will use AI assistants in your work. You hand it in before you leave. It counts toward professional practice.

**Files:** [the template](../Resources/AI-USAGE-CHARTER-TEMPLATE.md)

**Data:** none.

## Instructions

1. Open the template and copy it into a document of your own.
2. Write one or two sentences for each of the five commitments. Use your own words and your own field: a bank, a hospital, a shop, a university.
3. Read it once as a client would. Would it make them trust you more? Change any line that is vague.
4. Add your name and the date.
5. Hand it in the way your instructor says in class.

| # | The commitment | It starts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What I never paste | "I will not put … into a prompt." |
| 2 | How I check | "Before I use an answer, I will …" |
| 3 | When I disclose | "I will tell … when …" |
| 4 | Which tools I use | "I will use only tools that …" |
| 5 | What stays my decision | "I will not let an assistant decide …" |

## Check yourself

- All five commitments are filled in, and none is copied from the slide.
- Commitment 1 names at least the four things from class: client data, personal data, secrets, unpublished figures.
- Commitment 2 names a real check: a known total, one case by hand, or a second method.
- A classmate can read it in one minute and say what you will and will not do.

## Hint

Write it for one real situation you expect in your first job, not for every situation.

## Bonus

Add a sixth commitment for your own field.
