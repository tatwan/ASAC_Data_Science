# Starter pack: Tarek's D23 files

**These are Tarek's files, not yours.** Use them only to follow along in class if your own D23 work is not ready. Before you share your profile or project link with anyone, replace every file that came from here with your own screenshots, your own SQL and your own findings. A portfolio built from someone else's files shows nothing about you.

| Folder | What is in it | Use it in |
|---|---|---|
| [images/](images/) | `story-page.png`, the D23 story page (until Tarek adds his screenshot, a chart drawn from the D23 numbers and marked as a stand-in) | Activity 02, step 1: drag the whole `images` folder |
| [sql/](sql/) | `d23_bigquery.sql`, the full D23 SQL | Activity 02, step 2: open it, copy, paste |

To get the image onto your laptop: make a folder named `images`, open [images/story-page.png](images/story-page.png), click **Download raw file** (the download icon above the image), and save it into that folder.

## Make it yours afterwards

1. Finish D23 Activity 05, or rebuild the story page from your own D23 file.
2. Take your own screenshot, crop it to the report page, and name it `story-page.png`.
3. In your repository, open `images/story-page.png`, then **⋯ → Delete file** and commit. Upload yours with **Add file → Upload files** into the `images` folder and commit.
4. Reread your README: every number in it should be one you can find in your own work.
